GPU 计算性能提升。

## 更新

- **GPU 计算**：Geekbench 6 Vulkan 约 +24%，图像模糊与粒子模拟等计算任务接近翻倍。Mesa 编译器对计算着色器进行循环展开与读取合并，Turnip 对对齐的只读缓冲区读取使用宽格式。

本版实测：在平板上运行 Vulkan CTS 相关子集与 Geekbench 正确性校验；已装系统通过 APT 升级后重启，桌面、Firefox snap 与视频播放正常。

内核不变（`piano40`）。

## 已知限制

- Firefox 播放部分 HEVC 视频可能有轻微丢帧。AV1 使用软件解码，Chromium snap 不支持此硬解集成。
- 拔下 OTG 设备后，直接插入的 OTG 转接头可能不被识别；插拔一次充电器或电脑线，或改用扩展坞即可。
- 蓝牙偶发需要重新连接才能恢复数据或音频。
- 后摄为固定焦距，近景可能偏虚，颜色调校仍有限。

手写笔、指纹、休眠到磁盘及硬件时钟写入仍不支持。保留默认扬声器音量上限，开机后联网校时。

Improved GPU compute performance.

## Changes

- **GPU compute**: about +24% in Geekbench 6 Vulkan, with compute workloads such as image blur and particle simulation nearly doubling. The Mesa compiler unrolls compute shader loops and combines reads; Turnip uses wide formats for aligned read-only buffer loads.

Tested on the tablet: relevant Vulkan CTS subsets and Geekbench correctness checks; the installed system was upgraded through APT and rebooted, with the desktop, Firefox snap and video playback working.

Kernel unchanged (`piano40`).

## Known limitations

- Some HEVC videos may drop a small number of frames in Firefox. AV1 uses software decoding; Chromium snap is not covered by this acceleration integration.
- After an OTG device is unplugged, a plain OTG adapter plugged in next may not be detected; plug a charger or computer cable in and out once, or use a dock.
- Bluetooth occasionally needs reconnecting before data or audio works again.
- The rear camera has fixed focus; nearby subjects may be blurry and color tuning is limited.

Stylus, fingerprint, disk hibernation and hardware clock writes remain unsupported. Keep the default speaker volume cap and connect to the network to set the clock after boot.
